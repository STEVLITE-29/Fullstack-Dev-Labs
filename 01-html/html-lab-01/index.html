Bean Cake Recipe

This is a Bean Cake recipe based on the ingredients i use and how i prepare it.

Ingredients

  • Beans
  • Fresh Pepper
  • Onions
  • Salt
  • Maggi Cubes
  • Eggs
  • Groundnut Oil

Instructions

  1. Wash the Beans by removing the shell and leave to soak for about 30 minutes.
  2. Drain the Beans and blend it with the Fresh pepper, Onoins and Eggs.
  3. After blended till smooth, remove the mix from the blender.
  4. Season to your taste with the Salt and Maggi cubes.
  5. Fry in hot Groundnut Oil till golden brown
